NONUNIFORM RANDOM NUMBERS:
Many probability distributions are not uniformly random. Consider radioactive decay again, we stepped in time and asked if it decayed or not, and found prob of decay in some step dt. 

Now, what is the prob the atom survives until a given time t and decays between t and t + dt? this gives a nonuniform prob distribution. 
Chance of decay decreases exponentially in time. A fast way to do this is to just calculate a time at which each atom
decays. But to do so we need to sample from this nonuniform probability distribution. The way to see this is the transformation method.
