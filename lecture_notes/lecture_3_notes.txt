Two main things are in tension with comp algorithms: accuracy and speed

accuracy -- how close the computed number is to the analytical calculation
speed -- refers to the time (CPU time) it takes to perform the calculation

these are usually in tension, the better one is, the worse the other, must consider the answer

Machine Precision:

Floats are stored on a computer using a fixed number of bits, 
most comps are double precision now, 
sign: 1 bit, exponent: 11 bits, significand: 53 bits (52 stored), = 64 bits, 
so range is ~ 10^308 or accurate to about 15 sigfigs
good, but finite amount of precision

bring errors: Overflow and Underflow

Computer uses finite amount of bits, so y = 10*1e308 is saved as inf in python, Overflow Error
similarly in y = 10*1e-308 sets this to 0.0, Underflow error
code will still run, but a numerical limit, not correct analytical maths
python will just allocate memory to store integer until you run out

Rounding Error:
rounded of the number after a maths operation, can't check floata with x == 0.1, as it may be truncated etc, must check with 
    (abs(x-2.50<epsilon) # checking number is close to 2.5 and epsilon is your desired prec

because numbers are stored in binary, leads to round error etc. 
rounding error behaves like measurement in lab: 
    sigma = sqrt (sigma_1^2 + sigma_2^2)
good to have/know rounding error: 
error increases with root of sqrt of calcs done, Sqrt(N) fractional error that can become quite small
but ony works for numbers close in size and addition

subtraction can also lead to bad errors, numbers only saved to 15 places
consider e^-24 via the taylor series, we find
S(-24) vs e^(-24) gives an order of 4 magnitude difference - bad answer
rounding error from the large numbers and small numbers subtracted from eachother
so must think before: better to compute e(-1)**24 which gives a much better answer as S(-1) is well behaved

careless can lead to wildly different answers -- computer is always an approx to the actual answer, must always verify and check for accuracy 

see volume of a shell: relies on cancellation of two large numbers - BAD 
rewrite formula to avoid this - must implement math to avoid these errors, roundoff and subtraction errors, number are not math
